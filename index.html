From sensor to this page
  1. Globe probe — a DS18B20 inside a black globe measures the radiant heat.
  2. Online data — air temperature and humidity for the site are fetched from Open-Meteo every ten minutes.
  3. ESP32 — calculates the wet-bulb temperature and WBGT, sets the alert level and sounds the buzzer.
  4. Display & database — the same result goes to the device LCD and, within a moment, to the realtime database.
  5. This page — reads the database live; one record per minute is kept for the charts and the Excel export.

How WBGT is worked out

VI · Method

The wet bulb globe temperature is a heat-stress index of the surroundings. It combines three things the body feels at once: how hot the air is, how much sweat can evaporate, and how much heat arrives as radiation from the sun. Outdoors, with sunshine, the standard weighting is:

WBGT = 0.7 · Tnwb + 0.2 · Tg + 0.1 · Tdb

70 %
Tg globe

Tnwb — natural wet-bulb temperature: the cooling sweat can achieve, which depends on humidity. Tg — black-globe temperature: radiant heat from sunlight and hot surfaces. Tdb — dry-bulb, the ordinary air temperature.

Wet-bulb from air temperature and humidity (Stull, 2011)
Tw = T·atan[0.151977·(RH + 8.313659)½]
     + atan(T + RH) − atan(RH − 1.676331)
     + 0.00391838·RH3/2·atan(0.023101·RH)
     − 4.686035
T = air temperature in °C, RH = relative humidity in %

Where each value comes from: the globe temperature is measured by the station; air temperature and humidity are online values for the station’s coordinates; the wet-bulb temperature and WBGT are calculated. Online values are regional model data, so the result is an estimate rather than the reading of a calibrated WBGT meter.

Normalbelow 25.0 °CUnder the ACGIH action limit for moderate workContinue work
Caution25.0 – 27.9 °CAt or above the action limit (unacclimatised workers) for continuous moderate workStay hydrated
High risk28.0 – 31.4 °CAt or above the ACGIH TLV for continuous moderate work (acclimatised workers)Take regular breaks in the shade
Extreme31.5 °C and aboveAt or above the TLV for moderate work when only 0–25 % of the hour is workStop work, seek shade and water

The bands use the ACGIH screening values for moderate work. WBGT itself does not depend on the person; the worker’s task, fitness and clothing only decide which limit applies. This station is a screening tool for a student project, not a medical or safety-certified instrument.
